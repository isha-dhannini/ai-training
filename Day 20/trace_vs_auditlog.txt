Trace vs Audit Log – Ticket Analysis

Ticket 04 - Ticket Description: 
You are reproducing a failing request in staging and need to determine precisely where the error occurred during execution.

Suitable Option: Trace / Spans

Explanation: 
Traces and spans reveal the full execution path of a request, including service calls, dependencies, timing, and failure points. 
This makes them the right tool for locating the exact component or function responsible for the error. 
Audit logs are intended for recording business actions and decisions, not for step-by-step debugging of runtime failures.



Ticket 05 - Ticket Description: 
FinOps needs a live dashboard showing average token usage and cost per request, and sampling about 10% of traffic is acceptable.

Suitable Option: Trace

Explanation
Trace data can capture request-level details such as token counts, latency, and model usage. 
Because FinOps only needs aggregated token and cost metrics rather than a permanent record of every request, 
sampled traces are sufficient and far more efficient than storing everything in an audit log.



Ticket 06 - Ticket Description: 
A high-stakes business decision must stay available and verifiable for years, even if someone with database write access later attempts to 
alter the records.

Suitable Option: Audit Log

Explanation: 
Audit logs are designed as durable, append-only records for compliance and governance. They preserve what action was taken, who took it, 
and when it happened, making them suitable for legal review, accountability, and long-term verification.



Ticket 08 - Ticket Description: 
A guardrail blocked a suspicious transaction. Compliance needs a permanent record of the block, while the on-call team must investigate 
why the guardrail fired.

Suitable Option: Both – Trace / Spans + Audit Log

Explanation
- Audit Log provides the permanent compliance record showing that the transaction was blocked.
- Trace / Spans provide the technical execution details needed to understand how the guardrail was evaluated and why it triggered.
Using both covers the compliance requirement for long-term recordkeeping and the operational need for technical investigation.


Summary

Ticket 04 - Trace / Spans: best for debugging a failed request
Ticket 05 - Trace suitable for sampled token and cost monitoring
Ticket 06 - Audit Log: appropriate for immutable long-term decision records
Ticket 08 - Both: combines compliance evidence with technical debugging support
