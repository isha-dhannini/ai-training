1) INSURANCE – Claims adjudication
Chosen pattern: GraphFlow
Runner-up: Selector

Why:
The claim requires three independent checks — fraud screening, policy coverage, and medical coding review — that can run in parallel. After all three are complete, a final decision agent combines the results and decides approve or deny. This is a structured workflow with parallel branches followed by a merge step, which fits GraphFlow best.

Block diagram:

New Claim
   |
   +----------------+----------------+----------------+
   |                |                |
   v                v                v
Fraud Check    Coverage Check   Medical Coding Review
   |                |                |
   +----------------+----------------+
                    |
                    v
           Final Decision Agent
                    |
                    v
              Approve / Deny


2) MANUFACTURING – RFP response builder
Chosen pattern: GraphFlow
Runner-up: Round-robin

Why:
The response is built section by section in a defined order — technical, pricing, compliance, and timeline. After assembly, a reviewer checks the full draft and may send specific sections back for rework before final sign-off. Because the workflow has ordered steps plus review loops back to selected sections, GraphFlow fits best.

3) RETAIL – Buyer’s research assistant
Chosen pattern: Magentic
Runner-up: Selector

Why:
The merchandising team’s request is open-ended: find trending outdoor furniture materials and summarize supplier options. The subtasks are not fully known upfront and may require repeated web searches, supplier lookups, comparison, and refinement of results. Since the workflow is dynamic and tool-using rather than fixed in advance, Magentic is the best fit.
